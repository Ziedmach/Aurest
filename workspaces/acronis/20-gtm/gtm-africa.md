# Go-to-market: Africa

Draft 0.1, 1 October 2026. Covers North Africa (Tunisia, Libya, with Algeria and Morocco
opportunistic), Mauritania and francophone West Africa, and Rwanda and East Africa.

## 1. Market context

- Next Step assets: headquarters-level team in Tunis; offices in Tripoli, Nouakchott and
  Kigali; own sovereign cloud in Tunisia, Libya and Rwanda; SOC services for central banks
  and strategic institutions; delivery history in 15+ African countries.
- Acronis assets: data centers in Johannesburg (G2) and Lagos; European G1 data centers
  (Frankfurt, Strasbourg with HDS certification, Valencia, Rome) reachable from North Africa;
  distributors Ingram Micro (including Tunisia), Maxtec and 4Sight in southern Africa;
  local service providers in Tunisia and Morocco. No Tunisia-specific cloud distributor
  announced, so the route (direct under Next Step's tenant versus distributor) must be
  confirmed with Acronis.
- Demand drivers: central bank and telecom regulator requirements on continuity and data
  protection; ransomware incidents against banks, utilities and public bodies; rapid
  Microsoft 365 adoption; weak or untested backups; foreign-currency constraints that
  favour a local provider billing in local currency.
- Constraints: bandwidth to foreign data centers (favour Next Step local cloud for large
  datasets), payment and import regulations, French as the working language in most markets.

## 2. Segments and pains

| Segment | Key pains | Why Next Step with Acronis |
|---|---|---|
| Banks, microfinance, insurers | Regulator demands tested DR and data protection; branch endpoints exposed | DRaaS on Next Step cloud or Johannesburg, XDR with MDR, evidence reporting |
| Telecom operators and ISPs | Want to resell backup and security to their business customers | Sub-partner tenant, white-label, Next Step as operating layer |
| Public bodies and utilities | Sovereignty, local hosting, budget cycles | Next Step sovereign cloud in Tunisia, Libya, Rwanda; annual contracts in local currency |
| SMEs and ISVs (Tunisia, Rwanda) | No backup discipline, M365 unprotected, limited IT staff | Protect 365 pack per user per month, operated by Next Step |
| Local IT resellers and MSPs | Need a cloud line with local support and pricing in dinar, ouguiya or franc | Partner Launch pack, wholesale pricing, francophone support from Tunis |

## 3. Phase plan

**Phase 1 (October to December 2026): Tunisia and Libya from the installed base.**
- Protect Next Step's own cloud customers first: offer BaaS and DRaaS on Next Step cloud
  with Acronis as the engine (partner-storage SKUs, no G1/G2 uplift).
- Target 20 existing accounts from the Next Step customer list for the protection assessment;
  issue 5 proposals in TND and LYD (USD reference rate stated).
- French one-pager and proposal; webinar in French with Acronis France and Africa team.

**Phase 2 (January to June 2027): Rwanda, Mauritania and channel.**
- Kigali: offer to banks, telecoms and public bodies with Next Step cloud in Rwanda;
  Johannesburg as the Acronis-hosted alternative.
- Nouakchott: banks and public sector; recruit one local reseller.
- Recruit 3 sub-partners (one telecom or ISP, two IT resellers).
- Case study from a Tunisian bank or public body.

**Phase 3 (H2 2027): regional scale.**
- Morocco and Senegal through local systems integrators (pattern from the SonarQube plan).
- Cyber Frame Local in Next Step's Tunis and Kigali clouds for sovereign IaaS with protection
  built in.
- Participation in GITEX Africa (Marrakech) and Africa Tech events with Acronis.
