## Pricing principles (to formalise in the margin policy)

1. Sell per user or per workload per month; keep the customer price list short (one price
   per pack per unit, by tier of customer size).
2. Storage overage per GB per month is always a separate line.
3. The managed-service fee is explicit, so the platform price can follow Acronis changes
   without renegotiating the service.
4. Minimum term 12 months for SMB packs, 36 months for DR and sovereign packs.
5. Wholesale (Partner Launch) pricing sits between Acronis buy price and the retail pack
   price; the sub-partner keeps the customer relationship and margin above wholesale.
