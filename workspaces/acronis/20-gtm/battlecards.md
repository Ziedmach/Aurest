# Battlecards and objection handling

Short answers for sales conversations. Keep them factual; cite the source when challenged.
Competitor facts are summarised in `../00-context/competitive-landscape.md`.

## Objections

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "There is no Acronis data center in Saudi Arabia." | Correct. We offer Abu Dhabi (ISO 27001, IEC 22237, GCC region) for private-sector customers, and Next Step hosted storage in-Kingdom where NCA CCC or your regulator requires it. The proposal names the location and the controls. |
| "We already use Veeam / Commvault." | Keep it for the data center. We propose Acronis for endpoints, Microsoft 365, branches and subsidiaries, and 24/7 MDR, feeding your existing reporting. No rip and replace. |
| "Microsoft already protects Microsoft 365." | Microsoft keeps the service running; it does not provide long-term backup, point-in-time restore of a mailbox or SharePoint site, or protection against deletion and ransomware across your tenant. Microsoft's own Microsoft 365 Backup Storage is available through Acronis per GB where you prefer it. |
| "Our telecom bundles backup for free." | Ask for the last restore test report and the immutability setting. We provide quarterly tested restores, immutable copies, a DR runbook and a monthly report mapped to your framework. |
| "Acronis consoles are complex." | We operate the console. You receive a monthly report and a service desk; your team never needs to learn it. Our engineers are Acronis certified. |
| "Restores are slower than appliance-based solutions." | Full-system restores from the cloud take longer than a local appliance. For servers with a sub-15-minute RTO we propose Acronis Disaster Recovery with pre-provisioned recovery servers and tested failover, or a local Next Step Cyber Frame node, instead of an appliance you must buy and renew. |
| "Hosted storage is expensive." | Storage placement is a choice: Acronis-hosted for simplicity, Next Step hosted at lower cost, or your own storage. Bundles include 3 TB per server and unlimited Microsoft 365 storage. We show the overage line up front. |
| "We want to own the licences." | Available: Acronis Cyber Protect subscription licences through distribution, operated by us or by you. We will quote both routes. |
| "Price per user is higher than our current tool." | Compare the full scope: backup, DR, EDR or XDR, MDR, awareness training, operations and reporting in one fee, against three tools plus staff time. We can start with the modules the audit requires. |
| "Acronis support is slow." | Your support path is Next Step, 24/7, with named escalation into Acronis partner support. We hold the tier relationship. |

## Competitor quick cards

**Veeam.** Strength: enterprise VM backup, architectural control. Weakness for SMB and MSP:
needs infrastructure and skilled staff; no endpoint security. Our line: coexistence in the
data center, Acronis everywhere else.

**Datto (Kaseya).** Strength: appliance BCDR with instant local virtualisation. Weakness:
hardware cost and lock-in, reported 40 to 60 percent price premium and renewal increases.
Our line: no appliance, residency choice, monthly consumption.

**N-able Cove.** Strength: simple, storage-inclusive, cloud-first backup. Weakness: no
security stack, limited SaaS breadth. Our line: backup plus security plus MDR in one agent.

**Druva.** Strength: pure SaaS, dedupe, resilience guarantee. Weakness: enterprise-direct,
no MSP multi-tenancy, no endpoint security. Our line: regional operations, residency, security.

**Commvault Cloud.** Strength: broadest compatibility, cleanroom recovery. Weakness:
complexity and cost at mid-market. Our line: time to value and operated service.

**SentinelOne, CrowdStrike, Sophos.** Strength: EDR brand and SOC adoption. Weakness: no
backup or recovery; separate agent and contract. Our line: keep them where a SOC runs them;
add Acronis backup and recovery; for customers without a SOC, Acronis XDR with MDR by
Acronis TRU covers detection and recovery in one.

**Microsoft Defender and Intune.** Strength: included in E5. Our line: Acronis integrates
with Intune and Entra ID, augments Defender AV, and adds the backup Microsoft does not include.

**Telco cloud backup (STC, Mobily, Zain, Tunisie Telecom, local ISPs).** Strength:
bundled pricing, in-country hosting. Our line: evidence, tested restores, MDR, compliance
mapping, and Next Step hosted storage to match residency.
