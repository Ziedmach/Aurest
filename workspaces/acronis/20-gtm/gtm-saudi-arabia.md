# Go-to-market: Saudi Arabia

Draft 0.1, 1 October 2026. Follows the structure Next Step used for prior vendor GTM plans:
segments and pains, phased plan, value proposition by persona, packs, enablement kit, KPIs.

## 1. Market context

- Regulatory pull: NCA ECC-2:2024 (domain 2 Defense, domain 3 Resilience including backup
  and recovery management, domain 4 Third-party and cloud), SAMA CSF for financial
  institutions, CMA for capital markets, PDPL for personal data, NCA CCC for cloud and
  residency, and the SME-oriented NCA segment frameworks. Regulators expect evidence.
- Threat context: phishing and ransomware remain the leading causes of incidents in the
  region (Acronis TRU H2 2025 report: email attacks up 16 percent per organisation).
- Market structure: large enterprises and government standardise on Veeam, Commvault and
  enterprise EDR with in-Kingdom SOCs; SMEs and mid-market rely on telco bundles, local
  MSPs or nothing tested. Microsoft 365 adoption is high and mostly unbacked.
- Residency: no Acronis data center in Saudi Arabia. Abu Dhabi (G2) satisfies most
  private-sector customers; government, finance and critical infrastructure need in-Kingdom
  hosting, served by Next Step hosted storage in a Saudi data center.

## 2. Segments and pains

| Segment | Key pains | Why Next Step with Acronis |
|---|---|---|
| Regulated mid-market (banks, insurers, capital-market firms, healthcare groups, 250 to 3,000 users) | Audit findings on backup testing and DR; Microsoft 365 unprotected; no 24/7 detection outside a SOC | Evidence-ready backup and DR, MDR by Acronis TRU, compliance mapping to ECC and SAMA, Next Step ISO 27001 operations |
| SMEs under NCA segment frameworks (20 to 250 users) | Must show basic hygiene and reporting; no IT team; ransomware fear | Ultimate Protection as one monthly fee per user, Next Step operates it, monthly report for the regulator |
| Subsidiaries and branches of large groups | Group standard (Veeam, Commvault) too heavy for sites; local IT thin | Coexistence: Acronis for sites, endpoints and M365, feeding the group's reporting |
| MSPs and IT resellers serving SMEs | Want a backup and security line without building a platform; need local billing and support | Sub-partner tenant under Next Step, white-label, wholesale pricing, pre-sales, tier-2 support |
| Government and semi-government (via tenders) | Licence ownership, in-Kingdom data, local content | Reseller route through the distributor, Next Step hosted storage, Saudi delivery team |

## 3. Phase plan

**Phase 1 (October to December 2026): launch from the compliance funnel.**
- Add a "resilience and endpoint" module to the NCA ECC, SAMA and CMA digital assessments
  so every assessment produces an Acronis-shaped gap list.
- Offer the one-week protection assessment to 10 assessed accounts; issue 5 proposals.
- Co-branded webinar with Acronis on ECC domain 3 evidence; GITEX Global presence in
  December with the Acronis stand if available.
- Recruit the first Riyadh MSP sub-partner.

**Phase 2 (January to June 2027): mid-market and channel.**
- Account-based campaigns in banking, insurance and healthcare with the compliance
  mapping; register all opportunities with Acronis.
- Partner pack and quarterly enablement for MSPs; target 5 sub-partners.
- Etimad tenders: respond with the reseller route where licence ownership is required.
- Case study from the first Saudi customer (with consent).

**Phase 3 (H2 2027): scale.**
- In-Kingdom hosted storage offer formalised with a Saudi data center partner.
- Cyber Frame-based sovereign IaaS with backup and DR built in, for customers leaving
  on-premises infrastructure.
- #TeamUp sports co-marketing evaluation; Black Hat MEA presence.

## 5. Offer packs
