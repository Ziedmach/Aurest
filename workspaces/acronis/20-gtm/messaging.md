# Messaging framework

Voice: calm, executive, precise. Sentence case. Numbers with units. No hype.

## Positioning statement

For regulated organisations and growing businesses in Saudi Arabia and Africa that must
prove they can recover from an attack, Next Step Middle East delivers cyber protection as a
managed service on Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud: backup, disaster recovery, Microsoft 365
protection and 24/7 managed detection from one platform, with data kept where your
regulator requires and evidence reported every month. Unlike point tools or telco bundles,
we operate it, test it and stand behind it as an ISO 27001-certified regional provider.

## Headline options

- One platform to back up, secure and recover every workload, with your data kept in region.
- Resilience evidence for NCA ECC domain 3, delivered as a service.
- Backup you can prove. Security that responds. Recovery you have tested.
- Secure. Scalable. Sovereign. Now with cyber protection as a service.

## Segment lines

- **Regulated mid-market:** "Regulators expect evidence, not intentions. We give you tested
  restores, mapped controls and a monthly report your auditor can read."
- **SME:** "Everything protected for one monthly fee per user. We run it; you get the report."
- **MSP and reseller:** "Your brand, our platform and engineers, live in 30 days."
- **Africa public sector and banks:** "Your data stays in the country, operated by a
  certified regional provider, billed in your currency."

## Words to use and avoid

Use: protect, recover, evidence, tested, sovereign, in-region, managed, accountable,
one platform, monthly.
Avoid: revolutionary, best-in-class, seamless, cutting-edge, military-grade, unbreakable,
any exclamation mark, any emoji.
