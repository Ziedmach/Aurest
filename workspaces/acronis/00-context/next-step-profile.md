  4 Third-party and cloud), SAMA CSF, CMA cybersecurity requirements, PDPL (SDAIA/NDMO),
  NCA CCC (cloud and data residency); planned coverage of NCA CSCC, OTCC, DCC, TCC and

  to ECC, NIST CSF and ISO 27001: Tamkeen (governance), Himaya (defense), **Mana'a
  (operational resilience: backup, DRaaS, high availability, cloud continuity)**, Thiqah
  (trusted ecosystem), Riyada (leadership and innovation). Acronis belongs in Mana'a first,
  with Security + RMM and MDR extending into Himaya.
- **Compliance-to-Pipeline (C2P)** motion with GO Telecom: Engage → Assess → Identify →
  Translate → Act → Pipeline. Live digital assessments for NCA ECC, SAMA and CMA. Backup,
  DR and resilience gaps found in assessments are the natural feed for Acronis opportunities.
- Procurement: public tenders through Etimad with two envelopes (technical, financial); SAR
  pricing with 15 percent VAT shown separately; 90-day validity; local-content attention
  (Saudi nationals in key staff tables).

## Partnership and co-sell pattern (GO Telecom deck)

Deck flow: cover → corporate profile → collaboration in action (four motions) → numbers →
from collaboration to strategic alliance (three pillars and governance). Four joint motions:
Compliance to Pipeline; co-sell, upsell and cross-sell inside partner accounts; partner sales
team enablement; joint offering development (technology → technical validation → business
case → commercial packaging → joint offering → launch). Governance ladder: executive
sponsors → quarterly steering committee → joint business plan → joint product and GTM
working group → executive pipeline review. Motto: "One Alliance. One Roadmap. One Revenue Plan."

This is the pattern reused in `50-co-sell/acronis-co-sell-plan.md`.

## Prior vendor GTM pattern (SonarQube deck)

Eight sections: title with geographic phasing; target segments and pain points table
(segment, pain, why the vendor); phase-1 country plan as a timeline (outreach, co-branded
workshops, 30-day POC campaign, local success stories); expansion markets and local SI
partnering; value proposition per persona; commercial packs; enablement kit (one-pager,
catalogue, demo and POC scripts, email and LinkedIn templates); phase-1 KPIs (10 leads,
5 POCs, 3 closed deals). Reused in `20-gtm/`.

## Document conventions

- Financial offers: cover block (client, submitted by, currency, VAT, classification, total,
  validity) → document control and confidentiality → commercial executive summary →
  summary table → detailed pricing by deliverable → payment milestones → pricing basis and
  inclusions → assumptions → exclusions → taxes, currency and validity → invoicing and
  payment terms (net 30) → change control → commercial acceptance with signature and stamp.
- Technical offers: cover letter, executive summary (outcome, our answer, what the client
  gets, why Next Step), understanding of requirements, proposed solution with bill of
  materials, compliance matrix, regulatory alignment table, four-phase methodology
  (Discovery and baseline → Architecture and design → Build and integrate → Assure and
  handover), governance and key staff, risks and assumptions, commercial summary, company
  profile and annexes.
- One-pagers: A4, English with Arabic mirror, header "Prepared for [Client] · [Month YYYY]",
  dark-green hero band, "What we deliver" six cards, "How we engage" four phases, numbers
  band (2,000 / 900 / 150 / 6 and the ISO line), call to action "Book a complimentary
  assessment".
- Tone: formal, outcome-led, evidence-oriented, numbers over adjectives, tables preferred,
  explicit assumptions and exclusions, bilingual-ready.
