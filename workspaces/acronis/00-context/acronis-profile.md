## What Acronis is for a partner like Next Step

Acronis sells almost entirely through partners. Its core product, Acronis Cyber Protect
Cloud, is built for service providers: one agent and one console covering backup, disaster
recovery, endpoint security (EDR, XDR, MDR), Microsoft 365 protection, email security, RMM,
PSA and now compliance and IaaS (Cyber Frame). The business model is monthly consumption
under a partner tenant, so the partner owns the customer relationship, the pricing and the
service wrap.

Three facts shape the Next Step play:

1. **Regional data centers exist in Abu Dhabi (opened April 2024, ISO 27001, IEC 22237) and
   Johannesburg (2022), plus Lagos (2022) and Tel Aviv.** No Saudi data center. Abu Dhabi is
   the in-region option for GCC customers; in-Kingdom residency needs Next Step hosted storage.
2. **The product is now a security platform, not only backup.** MDR by Acronis TRU went
   generally available in April 2026, GenAI Protection in April 2026, Cyber Compliance in
   September 2026. This lets Next Step sell one platform into the Himaya (defense) and Mana'a
   (resilience) stages of its Saudi maturity journey.
3. **Acronis is pushing an "autonomous IT" roadmap** (Cyber Console, Cyber Intelligence,
   Service Desk, Cyber Studio, Cyber Employee agents, July 2026). Useful for Next Step's own
   managed-services efficiency and for AI positioning, but treat as roadmap until generally
   available.

## Regional presence relevant to Next Step

**Middle East.** Dubai-based regional team under Ziad Nasr. Distributors: Mindware (cloud
distributor for GCC, Levant and Pakistan since 2022), Ingram Micro (MEA, including Tunisia).
Reported UAE growth: 68 percent year on year and 160 MSP partners (October 2024); cloud
billings ARR up 57 percent to June 2025 **[verify scope]**. Regional Partner Day UAE and
GITEX presence each year.

**Africa.** Data centers in Johannesburg and Lagos. Distributors and partners: Maxtec,
Teknov8, 4Sight Dynamics Africa; Metrofile Cloud named premier disaster-recovery partner at
Partner Day South Africa 2025. No Tunisia-specific cloud distributor announcement found;
Tunisia appears to report into the France and Africa organisation **[verify]**. Local
service providers exist (Oxahost in Tunisia, MCIMAROC in Morocco).

**France and Europe.** Office in Levallois-Perret; Strasbourg data center holds HDS
health-data certification; Frankfurt is the main EU region. Distribution: Watsoft (from
October 2025) and DSD Europe. About 1,000 recurring MSP partners and 200 traditional
resellers in France; cloud ARR up 50 percent in 2025.

## Acronis TRU (Threat Research Unit)

Acronis's research unit publishes monthly threat updates and a biannual Cyberthreats Report
and operates the MDR service. Use TRU statistics in Saudi and Africa messaging (phishing,
ransomware, AI-driven attacks) with the report cited, and name "MDR by Acronis TRU" exactly.
