# Competitive landscape for Next Step's Acronis offers

Who Next Step meets in Saudi Arabia and Africa, how Acronis positions, and where it is weak.
Battlecards with objection handling are in `../20-gtm/battlecards.md`.

| Competitor | Where we meet them | Acronis position | Honest weakness of Acronis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Veeam** | Enterprise and mid-market data centers, VMware-heavy estates; already a Next Step partner | One agent and console for backup and security; simpler packaging for SMB and MSP; no infrastructure to run for the customer | Veeam gives more architectural control for large VM estates; Acronis restores are slower on full systems; console learning curve |
| **Datto / Kaseya** | MSP channel, appliance-based BCDR | Software-defined, no appliance lock-in, per-workload or per-GB flexibility; Datto reported 40 to 60 percent more expensive with renewal increases | Datto instant virtualisation and screenshot verification give the fastest local RTO |
| **N-able Cove** | MSP channel, cloud-first backup | Acronis adds security, RMM, PSA and storage-destination choice; Cove has no security stack and limited SaaS breadth | Cove is simpler and storage-inclusive per device; often cheaper for pure backup |
| **Druva** | Mid-market and enterprise SaaS backup | Integrated security plus MSP multi-tenancy; Druva is direct and enterprise oriented | Druva 100 percent SaaS on AWS with global dedupe and a resilience guarantee; very high peer ratings |
| **Commvault Cloud (Metallic)** | Enterprise, regulated, broad platform coverage | Lower complexity, faster time to value, MSP fit | Commvault compatibility matrix and cleanroom recovery are unmatched for large enterprises |
| **Barracuda** | Email security and M365 backup for MSPs | Acronis email security (Perception Point) scored highest in a 2026 benchmark and bundles with backup and EDR | Barracuda stronger for email continuity and account-takeover defense |
| **SentinelOne, CrowdStrike** | Endpoint security tenders, mature SOCs | One platform for EDR, backup and recovery; MDR by Acronis TRU included at MSP price points | Thinner EDR brand recognition and MITRE track record; enterprises with a SOC standardise on these |
| **Microsoft Defender and Intune** | Every Microsoft 365 customer | Microsoft does not back up Microsoft 365 by default; Acronis integrates with Intune and Entra ID and augments Defender AV; Ultimate 365 bundle | Defender is near-zero marginal cost for E5 customers; sell Acronis as backup and MDR around Defender, not against it |
| **Sophos** | Mid-market endpoint and MDR; also a Next Step partner | Backup and DR integration in the same agent | Strong brand and MDR reputation; keep Sophos for customers who already run it and sell Acronis backup alongside |
| **Local and regional** | Saudi: STC, Mobily and Zain cloud backup services, local MSSPs; Tunisia: 3S, Focus, RFC; Africa: Metrofile Cloud (South Africa), telco cloud offers | Next Step's regional presence, ISO 27001, NCA ECC alignment and sovereign cloud; Acronis platform depth | Telcos bundle backup into connectivity contracts at low price; in-Kingdom hosting by telcos is a residency advantage we must match with Next Step hosted storage |

## Positioning rules

1. **Never sell against Veeam or Commvault in an enterprise data center that already runs
   them.** Sell Acronis for the endpoints, Microsoft 365, branch sites, subsidiaries and the
   MSP channel; propose coexistence.
2. **Against Microsoft, sell with Microsoft.** Acronis protects Microsoft 365 data Microsoft
   does not back up and adds 24/7 MDR for customers without a SOC. Use the Acronis for
   Microsoft CSPs alignment.
3. **Against telco bundles, sell evidence.** Tested restores, immutable backups, DR runbooks,
   regulator-ready reports, and Next Step's service levels.
4. **Against appliance BCDR, sell no-appliance economics and residency choice.**
5. **In Africa, sell sovereign and local-currency.** Next Step cloud in Tunisia, Libya and
   Rwanda, Johannesburg for southern Africa, billing in local currency, francophone support.
