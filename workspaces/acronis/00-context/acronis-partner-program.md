# Acronis Cyber Partner Program — what we know, what to confirm

Compiled 1 October 2026 from public sources. The program was restructured in May 2026 and
the details that matter most (discount percentages, MDF caps, credit values) are only in the
Partner Portal. Treat this file as the checklist for the first call with the Acronis channel
manager.

## Structure

- **One program** for MSPs, VARs, OEMs and hybrid partners; also covers cloud distributors,
  hosters, ISVs, systems integrators and telecoms. Launched as #CyberFit Partner Program in
  February 2021, now branded Acronis Cyber Partner Program.
- **Tiers:** Registered → Authorized → Gold → Platinum. Placement by a partner score
  (profile completeness, certifications, joint business plan, co-marketing engagement). At
  launch there was no revenue threshold for Gold, to ease the reseller-to-MSP transition
  **[verify current thresholds]**.
- **May 2026 restructure:** benefits, training and sales resources tied to platform usage;
  a consumption-based credit system redeemable for services and benefits, including AI
  capabilities **[verify mechanics]**.

| Tier | Benefits reported publicly |
|---|---|
| Registered | Discounts, training, solution and technical support |
| Authorized | Deeper discounts, marketing automation tools, NFR licences, partner account manager |
| Gold | Larger discounts, quarterly rebates, proposal-based MDF, dedicated account manager, #TeamUp sports benefits |
| Platinum | Best discounts, enhanced support, dedicated team and marketing support; MDF earned as a percentage of sales on quarterly target attainment |

## Mechanics

- No entry fee or minimum. Join by meeting minimum certification requirements.
- **Deal registration** through the Partner Portal adds discount on registered opportunities;
  the uplift rises by tier. Register every mid-market and enterprise opportunity early.
- **NFR licences** for the partner's own protection and for demo environments.
- **Acronis Academy**: sales and technical certifications needed to keep tier compliance
  and unlock benefits; MSP Academy PRO courses. Acronis claims Academy-trained partners grew
  revenue about 60 percent faster.
- **Partner Portal**: campaigns-in-a-box, nurture flows, co-brandable banners and landing
  pages, pricing and positioning guides, demo environments.
- **Referral program**: commission on Acronis products the partner does not deliver itself.
- **Quarterly performance rebates** for partners meeting assigned revenue targets.

## Two commercial routes Next Step can use

| | Service provider (Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud) | Reseller (Acronis Cyber Protect, self-managed) |
|---|---|---|
| Who bills the customer | Next Step | Next Step (licences sourced via distributor) |
| Billing basis | Monthly consumption under Next Step's tenant; per workload or per GB; commitment tiers 250 to 15,000 USD per month | Subscription licences, 1 to 3-year terms (subscription-only since October 2021) |
| Where data lives | Acronis DC (Abu Dhabi, Johannesburg, G1 regions), Next Step storage, or customer storage | Customer's own infrastructure |
| Fit | Default for SMB, MSP channel, managed services, Microsoft 365 protection | Customers who must own licences (public tenders, air-gapped environments), on-premises mandates |
| Current version | Continuous cloud releases | Acronis Cyber Protect v17 (Q2 2026): Windows on ARM, agentless Proxmox VE, macOS 26, RBAC |

Other routes: Acronis for Microsoft CSPs program (bundle with Microsoft 365 licences Next
Step already sells) and the Azure Marketplace listing (useful where a customer has Azure
committed spend).

## Co-marketing available

- MDF (proposal-based from Gold; percentage of sales at Platinum).
- Campaign-in-a-box content and co-brandable assets from the Partner Portal.
- #TeamUp: partner delivers Acronis to a professional sports property and gets co-branding
  and hospitality. Worth exploring with a Saudi Pro League club or a Rwandan or Tunisian
  federation once the business is established.
- Events: Acronis Accelerate (virtual), MSP Global and TRU Security Summit (October),
  regional Partner Days (UAE, South Africa), GITEX Global.
- Ecosystem: Microsoft CSP alliance, Auvik (network monitoring in Acronis RMM, 2027),
  RoboForm MSP partnership.

## Questions for the Acronis channel manager (first meeting)

1. Which tier is Next Step placed in today, and what is the path and timeline to Gold?
2. Discount schedule by tier and by route (service provider versus reseller via distributor).
3. Deal-registration rules: minimum deal size, protection period, uplift percentage.
4. MDF: proposal process, caps, eligible activities, claim deadlines.
5. Credit system (May 2026): how credits accrue and what they buy.
6. NFR entitlement: number of workloads, storage, services (including MDR) for demo and
   internal use.
7. Certification plan: which Academy courses and how many people for the tier.
8. Distributor of record for Saudi Arabia, UAE, Tunisia, Libya, Mauritania, Rwanda.
9. Named partner account manager and technical pre-sales contact for MEA and for Africa.
10. Joint business plan template and the cadence of quarterly reviews.
