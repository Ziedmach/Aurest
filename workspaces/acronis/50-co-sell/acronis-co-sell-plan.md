# Joint business plan with Acronis — Next Step Middle East

Draft 0.1, 1 October 2026, for the first channel-manager meeting. Follows the Next Step
alliance pattern (joint motions, numbers, governance) used with GO Telecom.

## 1. Objective

Make Next Step Acronis's reference service-provider partner for Saudi Arabia and francophone
Africa within 18 months: Gold tier, three sub-partners, and a referenceable regulated-sector
customer in each region. "One alliance, one roadmap, one revenue plan."

## 2. What each side brings

| Next Step brings | Acronis brings |
|---|---|
| Riyadh, Tunis, Tripoli, Nouakchott, Kigali, Nice offices; 900+ active clients; ISO 27001 and NCA ECC-aligned operations | Platform, Abu Dhabi and Johannesburg data centers, MDR by Acronis TRU |
| Compliance-to-Pipeline funnel (NCA ECC, SAMA, CMA assessments) and GO Telecom alliance in Saudi Arabia | Dubai regional team, distributor relationships (Mindware, Ingram Micro), Partner Portal campaigns |
| Own sovereign cloud in Tunisia, Libya, Rwanda; hosted storage option for in-Kingdom residency | NFR licences, Academy certifications, deal registration, MDF, #TeamUp |
| Microsoft, Fortinet, Cisco, Red Hat, Sophos, Secureworks relationships to position Acronis alongside | Analyst recognition, TRU research, event presence (GITEX, MSP Global, Partner Days) |

## 4. Asks of Acronis (first meeting)

1. Tier placement and a documented path to Gold with the certification and revenue milestones.
2. Deal registration on the first 10 opportunities, with the protection window stated.
3. NFR entitlement covering Next Step's own estate and a demo tenant with MDR enabled.
4. MDF or credits for: the assessment campaign, the French and Arabic one-pagers, two
   webinars, GITEX co-exhibition.
5. A named partner account manager and a pre-sales engineer for MEA and for France and Africa.
6. Clarity on the distributor route for Saudi Arabia and for Tunisia, Libya, Mauritania and Rwanda.
7. Regional references and permission to cite the Abu Dhabi data center certifications.
8. Early access to Cyber Compliance and Cyber Frame for the sovereign hosting design.

## 5. Commitments Next Step offers

1. Four certified people within 60 days; Gold-level certifications within 12 months.
2. Monthly commitment tier aligned to the forecast; quarterly review.
3. A joint business plan with quarterly targets and a shared pipeline report.
4. Acronis-branded presence in Next Step's assessments, events and proposals.
5. Case study participation once the first regulated customer is live.
