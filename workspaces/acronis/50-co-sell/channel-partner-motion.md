# Channel partner motion — MSPs, IT resellers and telecoms under Next Step

Next Step acts as the regional service-provider layer: partners sell under their brand,
Next Step hosts the sub-partner tenant, bills wholesale in local currency, and provides
pre-sales, tier-2 support and enablement.

## Partner profile

Managed service providers, IT resellers and telecom or ISP business units with 20 or more
SME customers, selling Microsoft 365, connectivity or managed IT, without their own backup
and security platform. First targets: one in Riyadh, one in Tunis, one in Kigali.

## What the partner gets

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Platform | White-label sub-partner tenant on Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud under Next Step; own customer tenants, own branding |
| Pricing | Wholesale price list per unit per month in SAR, TND, RWF or USD; the partner sets retail |
| Data placement | Abu Dhabi, Johannesburg, Europe or Next Step cloud, chosen per customer |
| Support | Next Step tier-2 and escalation to Acronis; partner handles tier-1 |
| Pre-sales | Sizing help, proposal template, demo tenant, joint customer calls for the first five deals |
| Enablement | Onboarding in 30 days; quarterly technical and sales sessions; access to the partner pack |
| Marketing | Co-branded one-pager, campaign content, joint webinars; MDF pass-through where Acronis approves |
| Commercials | Partner agreement with Next Step; monthly invoicing on consumption; minimum monthly amount to be defined; quantities reconciled monthly |

## Partner onboarding (30 days)

1. Agreement signed; sub-partner tenant created; branding applied.
2. Partner's own estate protected with NFR-style internal pricing (first reference).
3. Two partner engineers trained (half-day technical session, Acronis Academy enrolment).
4. First customer tenant created with Next Step engineers alongside.
5. Wholesale price list and retail guidance delivered; proposal template customised.
6. Monthly reporting and invoicing cycle started.

## Rules of engagement

- Partner-sourced deals belong to the partner; Next Step does not approach the partner's
  customers directly.
- Next Step-sourced deals in a partner's territory may be delivered through the partner by
  agreement.
- Deal registration with Acronis is handled by Next Step on the partner's behalf.
- Partners must keep tier-1 support and customer communication; Next Step provides the
  service-level backing.

## Partner pack contents

Partner one-pager, wholesale price list, onboarding playbook, proposal and one-pager
templates for the partner's customers, demo script, objection handling, co-branded campaign
content, support and escalation guide.
